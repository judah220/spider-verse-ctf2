📋 Conduct & Scoring

Rules of the Verse

Rules

  • Each team must register an account on the CTFd platform.
  • One flag submission attempt per challenge at a time.
  • No attacking the CTFd platform infrastructure.
  • No sharing flags or solutions with other teams.
  • All challenges are solvable from the provided PCAP files only.
  • No live network scanning required or permitted.
  • Treat other participants and organizers with respect.
  • Decisions of organizers are final.

Scoring

  • Points are fixed per challenge (not dynamic/decaying).
  • Mission 01 — Peter Parker's Trail: 100 pts
  • Mission 02 — Green Goblin's Trap: 150 pts
  • Mission 03 — Electro's Signal: 100 pts
  • Mission 04 — Mysterio's Network: 200 pts
  • Mission 05 — Doc Ock's File: 200 pts
  • Total possible: 750 points.
  • Tiebreaker: earliest last correct submission wins.
🚀 Getting Started

How to Enter the Verse

01

Register on CTFd

Click ENTER THE VERSE or visit http://localhost:8000/register. Create your account with a team name and password.

02

Install Wireshark

Download Wireshark from wireshark.org (Windows/Linux/macOS). It's free and is all you need to open .pcap files.

03

Download a Challenge PCAP

Open a challenge on the CTFd platform. Download the attached .pcap file to your local machine.

04

Investigate & Find the Flag

Open the PCAP in Wireshark. Use filters, stream following, and packet inspection to locate the hidden flag (format: FLAG{...}).

05

Submit Your Flag

Enter the flag in the CTFd challenge page. Points are awarded immediately on correct submission.
