── MISSION BRIEFING ──────────────────────────────────────────

Peter Parker intercepted a mysterious transmission on port 9001
before vanishing into the multiverse. He managed to snag a
packet capture before the portal closed.

The secret he sent is hidden in plain sight — buried inside the
network stream. Your mission: trace every packet, follow the
thread, and recover what Peter transmitted.

── TECHNICAL OBJECTIVE ───────────────────────────────────────

Analyze the attached PCAP file. A TCP conversation takes place
on port 9001. Inspect the application-layer data exchanged
between the two endpoints.

── INVESTIGATION APPROACH ────────────────────────────────────

1. Open stream_capture.pcap in Wireshark.
2. Apply filter: tcp.port == 9001
3. Right-click any matching packet.
4. Select: Follow → TCP Stream
5. Read the conversation carefully.
