CATEGORY  : ICMP / Network Forensics
DIFFICULTY: Easy
POINTS    : 100

── MISSION BRIEFING ──────────────────────────────────────────

Electro has been sending signals across the multiverse, hidden
inside what looks like ordinary network pings. To everyone else
it looks like routine ICMP traffic. But you know better.

Every ping carries a secret.

── TECHNICAL OBJECTIVE ───────────────────────────────────────

Analyze the ICMP packets in the attached PCAP file. A series of
echo requests are sent between two endpoints. The secret is
embedded in the data payload of those pings.

── INVESTIGATION APPROACH ────────────────────────────────────

1. Open icmp_secret.pcap in Wireshark.
2. Apply filter: icmp
3. Click on an ICMP Echo Request packet (Type 8).
4. Expand the "Data" or "ICMP" section in the packet details.
5. Look at the ASCII representation of the payload bytes.

Alternative: In the Packet Bytes pane (bottom), read the
ASCII column on the right side of the hex dump.
