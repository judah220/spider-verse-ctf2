CATEGORY  : Network Detection / Forensics
DIFFICULTY: Medium
POINTS    : 200

── MISSION BRIEFING ──────────────────────────────────────────

Mysterio has been running reconnaissance against our network.
Our IDS flagged an unusual traffic burst — the kind that makes
security engineers nervous. He came, he scanned, and somewhere
inside his recon traffic, he left evidence of what he found.

Your mission: unmask the attacker, identify the technique, find
the hidden service, and retrieve the flag from the conversation.

── TECHNICAL OBJECTIVE ───────────────────────────────────────

A PCAP file was captured during the intrusion. Analyze it to
answer the following questions:

  1. What is the IP address of the attacker?
  2. What scanning technique was used?
  3. Which service port was found open?
  4. What secret does the service on that port reveal?

── INVESTIGATION APPROACH ────────────────────────────────────

1. Open port_scan.pcap in Wireshark.
2. Filter for SYN-only packets:
     tcp.flags.syn == 1 && tcp.flags.ack == 0
   → This reveals the scanner's IP and the probed ports.

3. Look for successful connections (SYN-ACK responses):
     tcp.flags.syn == 1 && tcp.flags.ack == 1
   → Which port sent a SYN-ACK?

4. Follow the TCP stream for the open port.
   → Read the service banner carefully.

WIRESHARK FILTERS:
  - All TCP: tcp
  - SYN probes: tcp.flags.syn==1 && tcp.flags.ack==0
  - Open port traffic: tcp.port == <discovered port>
